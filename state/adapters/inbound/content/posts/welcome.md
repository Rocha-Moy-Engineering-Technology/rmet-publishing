This is where I publish writing about engineering and artificial intelligence:
short notes, longer articles, and the occasional paper.

## Three kinds of writing

The library is one collection with three kinds, because the difference is the
form rather than the subject.

- **Blog** — shorter notes and working thoughts. Written quickly, revised in
  place, and useful mostly because they are written down at all.
- **Articles** — longer pieces that develop one idea from beginning to end.
  They assume you have time and want the argument, not the summary.
- **Papers** — formal write-ups with an abstract, named authors, and a citation
  block. When a piece has a Digital Object Identifier (DOI) or an accompanying
  Portable Document Format (PDF) file, both are linked from the top of the page.

## Comments and reactions

Every piece ends with a comment section backed by GitHub Discussions. Sign in
with GitHub to leave a comment or add a reaction; the reaction bar is the way to
say "this was useful" without writing anything.
